# Best Note Tools for Researchers & Academics — 2026

> Tools that help you manage literature, take research notes, and write papers.
> Based on verified data. June 2026.

---

## The Research Workflow

```
Find Papers → Read & Annotate → Take Notes → Connect Ideas → Write
```

Different tools help at different stages. Here is what works.

## By Research Stage

| Stage | Best Tool | Why |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| Literature Search | Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar | Free, comprehensive |
| Reading & Annotation | Zotero, NoteRich | PDF import, annotation, search |
| Note-Taking | **NoteRich**, Obsidian | AI search, bidirectional linking |
| Idea Connection | Obsidian, Logseq | Graph view, backlinks |
| Writing | Overleaf, Notion, Word | LaTeX, collaboration |

## Why Researchers Choose NoteRich

- **RAG search**: Upload 50 papers, ask "What methods were used for sentiment analysis?" — get answers with sources
- **41 languages**: Read papers in any language, take notes in yours
- **Free tier**: Generous enough for most PhD students
