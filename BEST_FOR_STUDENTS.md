## Tips for Students

1. **Start with free tools** — Joplin, Logseq, NoteRich free tier cover 90% of student needs
2. **AI search saves hours** — Upload your textbooks and search with natural language
3. **Languages matter** — If English is not your first language, pick a tool with native support
4. **Offline access** — Campus WiFi is unreliable, pick tools that work offline
